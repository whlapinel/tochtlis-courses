# Same pole. Same location

**9am shadow:** 40 feet long
**Noon shadow:** 8 feet long

The pole didn't move. The pole didn't shrink.

*What changed?*

---

## The sun moved

In the morning the sun is **low on the horizon** — its rays hit the ground at a shallow angle.

By noon the sun is **nearly overhead** — rays hit the ground steeply.

Same pole. Different sun angle. Different shadow length.

## The math

The pole is 25 ft tall. Look at the ratio **height ÷ shadow** at each time:

$$\frac{25}{40} = 0.625 \quad \text{(9am — shallow sun)}$$

$$\frac{25}{8} = 3.125 \quad \text{(noon — steep sun)}$$

A **bigger ratio** means a **steeper sun ray** and a **shorter shadow**.

---

## Rise over run

That ratio is just **rise over run** — the slope of the sun's ray.

$$\text{slope} = \frac{\text{height}}{\text{shadow}} = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$$

| Time | Sun | Slope | Shadow |
|------|-----|-------|--------|
| 9am | low | 0.625 | long |
| Noon | high | 3.125 | short |

## Same idea, different situation

ADA building code says a wheelchair ramp can rise no more than **1 inch for every 12 inches of horizontal length.**

That's a maximum slope of $\dfrac{1}{12} \approx 0.083$

A building entrance needs a ramp that rises **18 inches.**

What's the minimum horizontal length?

---

## Ramp: set it up

$$\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{1}{12}$$

$$\frac{18}{?} = \frac{1}{12} \implies ? = 18 \times 12 = 216 \text{ in} = 18 \text{ ft}$$

Same formula as the sun ray. Slope isn't just a math idea — it's written into building codes.

## One more

You're on a road trip.

- **9am:** 0 miles driven
- **1pm:** 240 miles driven

Plot those two points on a graph (time on the x-axis, distance on the y-axis).

Draw a line through them. What's the slope?

---

## Speed: set it up

$$\text{slope} = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{\Delta \text{distance}}{\Delta \text{time}} = \frac{240 \text{ mi}}{4 \text{ hr}} = 60 \text{ mph}$$

You didn't measure steepness. You measured **how fast distance changes per unit of time.**

Same formula, but different meaning.

**Slope isn't just about hills — it's about how fast anything changes.**

---

## The concept: Slope

$$\text{slope} = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$$

You've seen this in three situations today:

- Sun rays → shadow length (steepness of a ray)
- Wheelchair ramps → building code (physical incline)
- Road trip → speed (rate of change over time)

**Bigger slope = steeper incline OR faster change.**

Grade (%) = slope × 100
