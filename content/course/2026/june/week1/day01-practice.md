**Problem 1 — Recipes**

A cookie recipe makes **24 cookies** and calls for **2¼ cups of flour.**

You want to make **60 cookies** for a party. How much flour do you need?

{{< problem id="p2" answer="28.57" tolerance="0.02" unit="dollars" hint="Set up: (1 dollar)/(17.50 pesos) = (? dollars)/(500 pesos). Solve for ?." solution="500 ÷ 17.50 ≈ $28.57" >}}
**Problem 2 — Exchange Rates**

The exchange rate today: **$1 USD = 17.50 Mexican pesos.**

You want to spend **500 pesos.** How many dollars is that?
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p3" answer="16.26" tolerance="0.03" unit="feet" hint="Set up: (friend's real height)/(friend's photo height) = (tree's real height)/(tree's photo height). Friend is 5.5 ft real, 2.3 in in photo." solution="5.5 × 6.8 ÷ 2.3 ≈ 16.26 feet" >}}
**Problem 3 — Photography**

You take a photo of your friend standing next to a tree.

- Your friend is **5 ft 6 in** tall in real life
- In the printed photo, your friend is **2.3 inches** tall
- In the same photo, the tree is **6.8 inches** tall

How tall is the real tree? (Answer in feet.)
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p4" answer="14" tolerance="0.01" unit="feet" hint="If ¼ inch = 1 foot, then 1 inch = 4 feet. Set up the proportion with 3.5 inches." solution="3.5 × 4 = 14 feet" >}}
**Problem 4 — Blueprints**

An architect's blueprint has a scale of **¼ inch = 1 foot.**

A bedroom wall measures **3½ inches** on the blueprint. How long is the real wall?
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p5" answer="107.69" tolerance="0.02" unit="gallons" hint="Set up: (6 gallons)/(156 miles) = (? gallons)/(2800 miles). Cross-multiply." solution="6 × 2800 ÷ 156 ≈ 107.7 gallons — about 7.2 full tanks of gas" >}}
**Problem 5 — Fuel**

A car travels **156 miles** on **6 gallons** of gas.

How many gallons would it need to drive from New York to Los Angeles — about **2,800 miles?**

*(A typical gas tank holds 15 gallons. Once you have the answer, figure out how many full tanks that is.)*
{{< /problem >}}
