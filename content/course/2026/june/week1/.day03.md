# You have a ruler and a working set of eyes.
# The building across the street is too tall to climb.
# You know how far away you are from it.

*That's it. Can you find its height?*

---

## The setup

Hold a ruler at arm's length (~24 inches from your eye).

Align it with the building.

Count how many inches of ruler the building "fills."

You need three things:
- **a** — your arm length (inches)
- **r** — how many inches the building fills on the ruler
- **d** — your distance from the building (feet)

---

## Why it works

Your eye → ruler → ruler measurement: a **small triangle**.

Your eye → building base → building top: a **large triangle**.

They share the same angle at your eye → **similar triangles**.

## The proportion

$$\frac{r}{a} = \frac{h}{d} \implies h = \frac{r \times d}{a}$$

**Example:** arm = 24 in, ruler reading = 3 in, distance = 80 ft

$$h = \frac{3 \times 80}{24} = 10 \text{ ft}$$

2. Arm = 20 in, ruler = 4 in, distance = 100 ft. How tall is the building?

## Want to try it? *(optional)*

Stand across the street from a house or telephone pole.

Estimate your distance (count paces — 1 pace ≈ 2.5 ft).

Do the ruler trick. Compare to any known measurements.
