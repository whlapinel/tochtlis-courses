**Problem 1 — Ruler Trick**

You hold a ruler at arm's length (**22 inches** from your eye).

The ruler reading for a flagpole is **4 inches.**

You are **150 feet** from the flagpole.

{{< problem id="p2" answer="18.86" tolerance="0.03" unit="feet" hint="Set up: your height / your distance = wall height / mirror's distance. Solve for wall height." solution="(5.5 × 12) ÷ 3.5 = 66 ÷ 3.5 ≈ 18.9 feet" >}}
**Problem 2 — Mirror Method**

You place a mirror flat on the ground **12 feet** from a wall.

You back up until you can see the top of the wall in the mirror. You are now **3.5 feet** from the mirror.

You are **5.5 feet** tall.

How tall is the wall?
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p3" answer="67.5" tolerance="0.02" unit="feet" hint="Set up: car length / car photo size = house length / house photo size. Solve for house length." solution="(15 × 9) ÷ 2 = 135 ÷ 2 = 67.5 feet" >}}
**Problem 3 — Satellite Photo**

A satellite photo shows a car (known to be **15 feet** long) as **2 mm** in the image.

A house in the same photo appears **9 mm** long.

How long is the real house?
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p4" answer="2091" tolerance="0.03" unit="miles" hint="Set up: ruler reading / arm length = moon diameter / moon distance. Solve for moon diameter." solution="(0.21 × 239000) ÷ 24 = 50190 ÷ 24 ≈ 2,091 miles (actual: 2,159 miles)" >}}
**Problem 4 — Moon Diameter**

Astronomers measure that the moon appears to cover **0.21 inches** of a ruler held at arm's length (**24 inches** from your eye).

The moon is **239,000 miles** away.

Estimate the moon's diameter in miles.
{{< /problem >}}

{{< problem id="p5" answer="90" tolerance="0.02" unit="mm" hint="Set up: film width / film distance = image width / screen distance. Solve for film distance." solution="(36 × 5000) ÷ 2000 = 180000 ÷ 2000 = 90 mm" >}}
**Problem 5 — Film Projector**

A 35mm film camera projects an image onto a screen **5 meters** (5,000 mm) away.

The film frame is **36 mm** wide. The image on the screen is **2,000 mm** wide.

How far is the film from the lens? *(Answer in millimeters.)*
{{< /problem >}}
